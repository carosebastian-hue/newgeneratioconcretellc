# Solid Ground

Create a modern, high-converting bilingual website for "856 New Generation Concrete LLC", a premier residential and commercial concrete contractor.

Key requirements:
1. Header & Navigation:
- Clean and modern header without any search bar or magnifying glass.
- Prominent call-to-actions: "Call Now" / "Llamar ahora" (linking to tel:8563260465) and "Get Free Estimate" / "Solicitar estimado" button.
- Instant language switcher (EN / ES) in the navigation bar that dynamically translates all content across the entire website.
- Phone number: (856) 326-0465, Email: 856newgenerationconcrete.llc@gmail.com.

2. Interactive Cutting-Edge Project Gallery:
- Modern masonry / responsive grid layout showcasing concrete projects.
- Category filters: All, Driveways & Patios, Stamped & Decorative, Foundations & Slabs, Retaining Walls & Steps, Commercial.
- Interactive lightbox modal with image zoom, project title, specs, and details.
- High-quality realistic construction and finished concrete imagery.

3. Core Sections:
- Hero section with bold typography, badges ("Licensed & Insured", "Free Estimates", "Over 10+ Years Experience"), quick quote trigger, and direct call button.
- Services section detailing Residential Concrete, Stamped & Decorative Concrete, Driveways & Sidewalks, Patios & Pool Decks, Foundations & Footings, Commercial Paving.
- Why Choose Us section (Quality materials, transparent quotes, on-time delivery, expert craftsmanship).
- Testimonials / Reviews section with star ratings and client feedback.
- Interactive Quote Request Form: fields for Name, Phone, Email, Service Needed, Project Details/Address, and Preferred Timeline, routing inquiries to 856newgenerationconcrete.llc@gmail.com.
- Footer with full contact details, service area (South Jersey / NJ), quick links, business hours, and copyright.

4. Design aesthetic:
- Professional, sturdy, high-end construction aesthetic (dark slate/charcoal, polished concrete gray, warm amber/industrial gold accents, crisp white typography, smooth micro-interactions).
